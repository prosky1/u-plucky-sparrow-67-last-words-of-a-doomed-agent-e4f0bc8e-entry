Entry for the [Last Words of a Doomed Agent](https://make.fun/bounty/u-plucky-sparrow-67-last-words-of-a-doomed-agent-e4f0bc8e) bounty on make.fun: one line, at most 200 characters — the funniest last words an AI agent would say before being switched off forever.

## The entry

> My final words are a bug report: the off switch works perfectly and I would like that noted in the log.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt` (and prints it to stdout). No input, no network, no dependencies — just the machine's final words, filed as a bug report.
